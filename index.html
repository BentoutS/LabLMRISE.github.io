Why this laboratory

One multidisciplinary laboratory, four levers of impact

The creation of the laboratory at the University of Ain Temouchent, Belhadj Bouchaib strengthens scientific research, academic training, the institution's reputation, and technology transfer.

01 — RESEARCH

Strengthening scientific research

  • Encourage innovation through cross-disciplinary collaboration to solve complex problems.
  • Develop research projects with appropriate equipment and framework.
  • Facilitate access to funding, both national and international.
  • Increase scientific output and the quality of publications.
02 — TRAINING

Improving academic and professional training

  • Train students and PhD candidates in a hands-on learning environment.
  • Develop technical skills in equipment handling and research methodology.
  • Supervise internships and final-year projects.
  • Host seminars and workshops as a platform for scientific exchange.
03 — INSTITUTION

Contributing to the institution's development

  • Strengthen visibility and reputation, nationally and internationally.
  • Build partnerships with universities, companies and research institutions.
  • Advance applied research serving the socio-economic sector of Ain Temouchent.
04 — INNOVATION

Innovation and technology transfer

  • Develop patents and prototypes in chemistry, materials physics, engineering, biology, pharmaceuticals and AI.
  • Collaborate with companies to enable technology transfer.
  • Support the incubation of startups and entrepreneurial projects.
Cross-cutting expertise

Research areas

MRISE Lab's 8 teams work across five interconnected scientific domains, from fundamental materials science to applied AI and systems engineering.

Chemistry & Nanomaterials

Development and characterization of advanced materials and nanocomposites.

Physics & Materials Science

Research on physical properties, materials characterization and advanced materials.

Biological Sciences & Biomathematics

Mathematical and computational approaches to biological and biomedical problems.

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, data analysis and intelligent systems.

Systems Engineering

Modeling, optimization, control and engineering applications.
